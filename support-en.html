Restore my subscription

On a new iPhone or iPad: open StitchMuse, tap Premium, then Restore Subscription, with the same Apple ID.

Cancel my subscription

iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → StitchMuse. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid being charged.

Free trial

If you cancel during the free week, you won't be charged.

Import a PDF pattern

Tap +, then Import a PDF pattern, and pick the file in the Files app. Patterns you bought by email can be saved to Files first.

Delete a pattern

On the home screen, press and hold a pattern, then choose Delete.
