Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026 · karnisalaheddine@gmail.com

In one sentence: StitchMuse collects no personal data, uses no servers and shares nothing with anyone. Your photos and patterns stay on your device.

01What StitchMuse stores

StitchMuse saves only what it needs to work, on your device: the patterns you create, the PDF patterns you import, your stitching progress and highlighting, and your settings. This data never travels over a network: StitchMuse has no server to receive it.

02Your photos

When you turn a photo into a pattern, the photo is processed entirely on your device, including the background removal, which uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. StitchMuse only reads the photo you choose; it never uploads it.

03What StitchMuse does not collect

No account is needed. StitchMuse does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, advertising identifiers or browsing data.

04No third-party tools or tracking

StitchMuse includes no analytics kit, no advertising SDK and no tracking tool. No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone.

05In-app purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. StitchMuse never sees or stores your payment information.

06Backups

If you use iCloud Backup, your device backup may include StitchMuse's data. That backup is managed by Apple under your iCloud account.

07Deleting your data

There is no account or server to delete. Delete a pattern in the app with a long press, or uninstall StitchMuse to erase everything it stored.

08Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the new version published on this page.
